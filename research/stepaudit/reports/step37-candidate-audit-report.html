9. Additional Insights

ROCmFPX is the body, not the repair

Best new signal is selective escape from FPX (blk44 down → q4_K), not more FPX everywhere. Direct FP4 replacement on the same tensor fell to 20-case 70.

K-side > V-side

AGENTK ≈ QP total (72); AGENTV collapses to 60. Attention key fidelity carries more of the tool-state signal than value alone.

Combining winners usually destroyed them

AGENTKV+blk44, blk44+lateKV, blk43+44 all regressed. Interaction space is hostile; prefer single-variable next steps.

Imatrix source matters

Step imatrix → blk44; Apex-era path → blk42. blk42’s 80 is still a real lift over QP hard-slice — not a waste signal.

MTP is a stability axis

n_max=1 helped blk44 stress; broader MTP did not create a weight win. Treat MTP knobs as runtime A/B, not as quant recipe proof.

Prune policy was too aggressive

Recipes/profiles remain for many deleted shards, but rebuild cost is high. Guardrail written after the damage: don’t final-reject/delete on one pass when same-route control is beaten.
