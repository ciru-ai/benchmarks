Protocol differences, first attempts and preserved failures

These saved records are not a single controlled head-to-head trial. Earlier Ciru IU4, Gufo and original HC-Q8 results come from the original comparison; Tom’s build is the October 12 qualification. Host policies, batches, run dates, warm-history and lifecycle differ.

Halogen 0.17.1 uses the stock 84-case aligned-tool-rules protocol; its race lane shows only TC70–84 from its first pass. This differs from the standalone corrected 15-case panel used for Tom’s build. The original scorers are preserved, including known TC34/TC40/TC80 caveats. No overall leaderboard or combined winner is inferred.

Tom’s Hermes run retains HA-17’s score of 70: delegated tools executed, but the merged summary failed its artifact check. Counting retains 17/18 clean first outputs; the 4096-input, empty-depth output skipped 75 and 76. A two-request fixture-mount failure is preserved as invalid harness diagnostics and excluded from the matching Hermes pass; it was corrected before that full 20-case pass.

The current native PP8192 result has one scored round after the required unscored native warmup. Historical Hermes records have two runs; this page replays their first runs. Every other selected first outcome is preserved without a model-outcome retry.
