Atlas vs BF16 Qwen3.6 27B

Inference comparison covering prompt processing, text generation, memory, wall time, MTP sweep behavior, and HermesAgent-20 outcomes on the Strix Halo class.

Summary Charts Speed tables MTP sweep HermesAgent-20 Evidence basis

Headline

BF16 has the stronger prompt-processing lane and lower loaded memory. Atlas reports higher decode throughput in the exact-token speed sweep, but BF16 wins the tested end-to-end speed prompts because prompt processing dominates these contexts.

324.02 vs 163.694k PP tok/s, BF16 compared with Atlas.
15.67 vs 11.304k TG tok/s, Atlas compared with BF16.
64.96 vs 98.27 GiBLoaded memory, BF16 compared with Atlas.
87 vs 82HermesAgent-20 score, BF16 compared with Atlas.
Selected BF16 MTP setting is nmax6. Atlas MTP setting is num_drafts=1 with K2 verification.
