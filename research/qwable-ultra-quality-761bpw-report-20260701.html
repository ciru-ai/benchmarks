Result Summary

The earlier Quality model is the public 7.37 BPW Strix Quality lane from the June ROCmFP6 report. It already matched the Unsloth Q6 baseline on HermesAgent-20 plus-pass count and slightly beat it on aggregate score in that run.

The UltraQuality lane is the newer 7.61 BPW default quality build: ranked leave-32 ROCmFPX with a Q6_K splice and a Strix ROCm MTP profile. It is not just a label change. The refresh evidence shows better MTP acceptance, lower KLD, and a higher HermesAgent-20 score than the superseded STRIX QUALITY row.

Interpretation: Ultra Quality is the quality-first lane. Use it when the goal is reducing tail risk and preserving high-impact attention behavior. Use 7.37 BPW Quality when the goal is the best published balance of quality evidence, size, and served speed.
