Published v2/v2.1 results measured Sep 25, 2026

Decode. Ciru ran the SparkDash prompt replay on V18 with DSpark. In the full-request view, Ciru’s rate divides 255 post-first tokens per stream by the entire request wall time. MiaAI’s side uses her published aggregate generation rate.

Prefill. Ciru’s chart uses the V19 cold sweep with 16K chunks and 1M context capacity. MiaAI’s chart uses the higher of the two v2.1 passes. Both Spark passes, exact token counts and Ciru’s first-token times are in the download.

Model. The Ciru and MiaAI model revisions have identical hashes for all 48 weight shards.

V19 prefill check. The INT8 path leaves the V18 decode path in place. On the 16K validation prompt, INT8 matched the FP16 top token and all 10 top candidates. The V19 and V16 retrieval answer token IDs also matched.

Full-context metric. Elapsed time covers the entire request. Ciru reported time to first token separately. MiaAI’s TP4 writeup summarizes its results as 322–325 s. The chart uses the raw log’s exact 325.2 s.
