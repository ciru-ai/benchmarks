08 / STATUS AND REMAINING VALIDATION

Correctness passed. Sustained throughput remains open.

The final benchmark package is complete for the selected engineering variant on the original cases. The broader 1,000 accepted profiles/hour goal is not established by this short session.

Established by the final package

  • Both saved batches retain all 44 required meanings across 42 fields with zero unsupported extras.
  • The separate original audit passes 56/56, and the original scorer confirms all IDs and decisions.
  • The source builder uses original inputs and static contracts, without gold or prior predictions choosing its candidates.
  • Fresh tokenization, model execution, projection and readback are measured in both warm batches; cleanup is recorded.

Not established by this run

  • Sustained 1,000 accepted profiles/hour, including an explicit policy for independent review.
  • Generalization to unseen sources or a new untouched evaluation set.
  • Per-person latency within the shared batch, production energy cost, or dollars per approved profile.
  • Performance on Ben’s M3 Ultra, or enforcement of a 16 GB memory ceiling.
