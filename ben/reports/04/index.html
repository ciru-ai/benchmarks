04 / What the recent tests taught us

Less output can help.
Lost meaning cannot.

These are bounded experiments on roles and evidence. Their improvements and failures help choose the next method; they do not establish a new profile-production rate.

A smaller representation

349 → 73

generated tokens in the compact role pilot

Replacing separate evidence slots with one source-ID group cut the output substantially. The compact run took 7.58 seconds, but only 2/5 required roles had usable complete groups.

Grouped slots · 34973

The compact approach also found all six role titles in the original cases, but only 2/6 groups carried complete role-and-institution evidence.

A simpler verification question

18 → 6

decisions in two successive six-group interfaces

Three checks per group scored 16/18 in 20.99 seconds. One complete-role question scored 6/6 in 10.72 seconds.

Three checks · 20.99 sOne check · 10.72 s

The producer representation and verifier question both changed. This is not a paired, quality-equivalent speedup; producer costs and full profiles are outside these clocks.

Cache reuse reduced work, but changed an answer.
A paired seven-line test reduced forwarded input from 4,529 to 701 tokens and lifecycle time from 11.875 to 8.120 seconds. Native input histories matched 7/7; outputs matched only 6/7. This is not a demonstrated quality-equivalent speedup.
Model time, lifecycle, and external elapsed
The E4B fixed audit’s model cohort is 9.9384 seconds, its model lifecycle 10.4644 seconds, and external elapsed 10.60 seconds. The historical Granite fixed-56 model cohort is 85.1427 seconds. Their cohort boundaries differ: Winnow includes tokenization/preflight; Granite’s cohort excludes initial rendering/tokenization. The normalized-role checker’s cohort is 7.433 seconds, while its external clock is 10.55 seconds. Never sum nested clocks.
What is outside the full workflow clock?
Runs start from previously captured pages. Live discovery and crawling, separate source-aware review, and sustained production validation are outside the profile clock. v6 plus its separate fixed-56 audit took 234.0955 seconds. New component results do not establish a replacement for the 79-claim verifier workload inside v6.

07 / Where we go from here
