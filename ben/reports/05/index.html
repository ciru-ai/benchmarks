08 / SCOPE AND NEXT STEPS

Status and limitations

Passing the benchmark is a measured development milestone. The next work must preserve that quality result while expanding evidence and reducing runtime.

Established on inspected cases

  • All 44 required profile meanings appear across 42 supported displayed fields, with no unsupported extras.
  • The separate original 56-claim audit has no false accepts or false rejects.
  • The recorded 4-bit, batch-4 replay takes 31.90 seconds under its stated timing boundary.
  • Source-derived proposals and focused decisions support zero generated answer tokens in the replay.

Still to demonstrate

  • The 18-second five-profile target with the same quality standard.
  • Accuracy and coverage on unseen cases and new source material.
  • Sustained throughput, repeatability and resource behavior across longer runs.
  • Performance on Ben’s M3 Ultra and readiness for production delivery.
